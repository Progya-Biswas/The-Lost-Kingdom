# Gameplay Guide and Sample Route

The following sequence describes the intended progression through the six-room world.

## Successful route
1. At the Arrival Courtyard, use TALK to receive Mira's clue.
2. Use MOVE NORTH to reach the Old Library; inspect the room with LOOK and collect the Ancient Seal with PICK ANCIENT SEAL.
3. Return to the courtyard with MOVE SOUTH, then travel east to the Greenhouse and collect the Luminous Key.
4. Follow the exits through the Sunken Hall to the Watchtower.
5. Speak to the Stone Guardian. Use the Luminous Key to open the guarded northern passage.
6. Enter the Hidden Vault and use the Ancient Seal to complete the final puzzle.
